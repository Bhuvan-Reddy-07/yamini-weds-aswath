Sacred Traditions

Customs & Rituals

Each Telugu wedding ceremony holds timeless spiritual significance, uniting two souls in sacred presence.

నలుగు (Nalugu / Haldi)

Purification & Glow

Sun, fragrant turmeric, aromatic oils, laughter and playful teasing among cousins to prepare the bride and groom for the sacred wedding day.

జీలకర్ర - బెల్లం & మాంగళ్యధారణం

The Divine Sumuhurtham

At 9:27 AM, placing cumin seed paste and jaggery on each other's heads signifies inseparable union, followed by tying the holy Mangalsutra.

తలంబ్రాలు (Talambralu)

Joyous Blessings

Showering pearls and sacred yellow rice over one another amidst cheerful music, celebrating prosperity, abundance, and eternal bliss.

మాంగళ్యం తంతునానేన మమ జీవన హేతునా
కంఠే బధ్నామి సుభగే త్వం జీవ శరదాం శతమ్

With this sacred thread, the foundation of my life, I tie this around your neck. May you live happily with me for a hundred autumns.
